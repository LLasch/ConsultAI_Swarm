# Case Brief: Surfboard Wax in Hawaii

**Company:** Hawaiian Surfboard Manufacturer
**Industry:** manufacturing

**Tags:** market_sizing, market_entry, hawaii, product_line_expansion, consumer_goods

## Current Situation

The client is an established surfboard manufacturer based in Hawaii with high brand recognition and a strong distribution network throughout the state. The CEO is seeking revenue diversification through product line expansion. Surfboard wax is a consumable product applied to surfboards to provide grip for surfers. The company has hired consulting support to conduct a market sizing analysis for Hawaii specifically.

## Key Challenges

A Hawaii-based surfboard manufacturer with strong brand recognition and distribution wants to expand its product line into surfboard wax. The company needs to determine the total addressable market size for surfboard wax in Hawaii to inform its entry decision.
