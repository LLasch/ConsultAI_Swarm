# Case Brief: AllHealthy CRM

**Company:** AllHealthy
**Industry:** healthcare
**Source:** Fuqua-Case-book-2014.pdf (pages 112–116)
**Tags:** crm, change_management, digital_transformation, healthcare, customer_experience, employee_adoption, it_implementation

---

## Current Situation

AllHealthy is one of the largest health plans in the United States with over 30,000 employees. The healthcare industry is undergoing drastic changes driven by healthcare reforms and increased competition from Health Care Exchanges. The company has decided to pursue customer-centric strategies to remain competitive. Currently, the contact center experiences significant operational inefficiencies due to fragmented case management and lack of call history visibility across associates.

## Key Challenges

AllHealthy's legacy CRM system lacks visibility into customer call history, forcing contact center associates to recreate cases and re-ask questions during transfers, which decreases customer satisfaction and employee productivity. The company must implement a new CRM system to support customer-centric strategies in response to healthcare industry reforms and the rise of Health Care Exchanges.

## Decision

AllHealthy decided to implement a new CRM system and hired a change management lead to develop and execute a comprehensive change management plan addressing stakeholder communication, change issues, training, and success metrics.

## Outcome

Not disclosed

## Discussion Questions

- Who are the key stakeholders in this CRM implementation and how should AllHealthy tailor its communication strategy for each group?
- What are the primary sources of resistance to this change and how can AllHealthy address leadership alignment, employee buy-in, and adoption challenges?
- How should AllHealthy measure the success of the CRM implementation across customer satisfaction, employee productivity, and business outcomes?
