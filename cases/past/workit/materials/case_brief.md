## Current Situation

WorkIt is a specialized insurance firm that exclusively sells workers' compensation insurance policies—a mandatory product that companies purchase to protect against litigation and unexpected costs from employee workplace injuries. The company has established success in three southeastern states (NC, SC, GA). Workers' compensation is a per-employee monthly premium model covering medical expenses, indemnity (lost wages), and related costs. The company has gathered market data on nine potential states and has set expansion criteria: target states must have between 30,000 cases (SC baseline) and 70,000 cases (GA baseline) of injured employees annually.

## Key Challenges

WorkIt, a workers' compensation insurance provider, is considering geographic expansion beyond its current markets in North Carolina, South Carolina, and Georgia. The company needs to determine whether expanding to a new state is strategically sound and, if so, which state represents the best opportunity.
