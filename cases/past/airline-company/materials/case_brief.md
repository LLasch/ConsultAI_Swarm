# Case Brief: Airline Company

**Company:** Major airline company (Brazil domestic)
**Industry:** aviation
**Source:** Haas_2006.pdf (pages 52–53)
**Tags:** aviation, capacity_optimization, revenue_growth, brazil, pricing, marketing_strategy, operational_efficiency

## Current Situation

A major airline operates domestic flights in Brazil with approximately 100 aircraft. The company serves three route categories: 60% short-haul flights between São Paulo and Rio (45 min, $100), 20% medium-haul between major capitals (90 min, $100), and 20% longer routes (3 hours, $300). The company maintains roughly 10% of its fleet in maintenance at any given time. Average ticket price across routes is $140.

## Key Challenges

The CEO wants to increase capacity by adding one more passenger per flight across the airline's fleet. The consulting team must determine whether this initiative is financially viable and develop a marketing strategy to attract these additional passengers.

## Decision

Not specified

## Outcome

Financial analysis shows adding one extra passenger per flight generates approximately $3.36 million in monthly incremental profit. Calculation: 800 flights/day × 30 days × $140 average ticket = $3,360,000. Marginal cost per additional passenger is negligible, making the full incremental revenue the profit gain.

## Discussion Questions

- What marketing strategies would most effectively attract one additional passenger per flight without cannibalizing existing demand or requiring price reductions?
- What operational constraints or implementation challenges might prevent the airline from realizing the full $3.36M monthly benefit from this capacity increase?
