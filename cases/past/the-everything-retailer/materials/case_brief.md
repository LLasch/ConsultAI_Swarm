# Case Brief: The Everything Retailer

**Company:** Everything Retailer
**Industry:** retail
**Source:** Fuqua-Case-book-2014.pdf (pages 81–90)
**Tags:** big-box retail, market saturation, store format innovation, new customer segments, domestic expansion, comp sales growth, grocery market entry

---

## Current Situation

Everything Retailer is a large US-based mass retailer comparable to Walmart, operating an extensive network of big-box discount stores across the country. Historically, the company experienced strong growth through store expansion. However, Exhibit 1 shows that while the company continued opening stores through 2014, sales growth has plateaued, indicating market saturation. The company's current cost structure is stable with no significant cost increases to address.

## Key Challenges

Everything Retailer is experiencing stagnating year-over-year sales growth of 1-2%, falling below the industry growth rate of 3% and far below the company's target of double-digit comparable sales growth. The core issue is that the current big-box store format has reached maximum market penetration, with new store openings outpacing sales increases, necessitating a strategy to tap into new customer segments.
