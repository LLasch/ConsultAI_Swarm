# Case Brief: Chickflix.com

**Company:** Chickflix.com
**Industry:** technology
**Source:** Wharton_2010.pdf (pages 94–97)
**Tags:** pricing, subscription_model, margin_analysis, dvd_rental, cost_structure

## Current Situation

Chickflix.com is a DVD rental subscription service targeting women, offering three tiered subscription models: 1 movie at a time ($9.99/month), 2 movies at a time ($14.99/month), and 3 movies at a time ($19.99/month). The company introduced a 1 movie at a time subscription model 1.5 years ago and has been growing its customer base and revenues per unit. Variable costs consist primarily of distribution (mailing) and content amortization (DVDs amortized over 30 rentals), while fixed costs are insignificant. The company faces no direct competitors in its niche market.

## Key Challenges

Chickflix.com is experiencing declining gross profit margins despite growing revenues across all subscription tiers. The company needs to identify whether the margin decline is driven by revenue mix shifts, rising costs, or competitive/market pressures, and determine how to reverse the trend.
