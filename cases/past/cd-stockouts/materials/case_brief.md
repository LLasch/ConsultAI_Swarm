# Case Brief: CD Stockouts

**Company:** Large multinational music company (post-merger American and European business)
**Industry:** technology
**Source:** Haas_2006.pdf (pages 36–36)
**Tags:** supply_chain, inventory_management, stockouts, music_industry, merger_integration, demand_planning, process_variability

---

## Current Situation

The company was formed through a merger between American and European music businesses several years prior. It holds the #2 market position with $75 billion in revenue. The music supply chain consists of four phases: Artists & Content, Physical Production, Distribution, and Retailers. While distribution and retail relationships are well-managed with good communication and 150-200 distributors, the Artists & Content phase lacks standardized procedures and operating guidelines.

## Key Challenges

A large music company (#2 player, 25% market share) experienced abnormally large stockouts during the holiday season, preventing retailers from replenishing CD inventory on store shelves. The case asks what went wrong and how to prevent future stockouts.
