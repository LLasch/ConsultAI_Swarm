# Case Brief: New England Retail Bank

**Company:** New England Retail Bank
**Industry:** finance
**Source:** Columbia_2006.pdf (pages 86–89)
**Tags:** profitability_decline, cross_selling, revenue_growth, retail_banking, product_penetration

---

## Current Situation

New England Retail Bank operates retail banking services across multiple northeastern U.S. states, focusing exclusively on retail banking without commercial banking operations. The bank maintains an efficient cost structure that benchmarks well against industry standards. The bank offers four main products: deposits (100% customer penetration), loans (50%), investments (7%), and life insurance (1%). Despite industry-leading cost efficiency, overall profitability has declined, suggesting the problem lies on the revenue side.

## Key Challenges

A profitable regional retail bank is experiencing declining profitability over the past year. The bank's management needs to diagnose the root cause and develop recommendations to reverse this negative trend.

## Decision

The bank's leadership decided to pursue a cross-selling strategy, specifically targeting investment products to existing customers as the primary growth lever. The CEO set an ambitious goal of generating at least $50 million in additional annual profit through this approach.

## Outcome

Analysis revealed that reaching the $50 million goal in one year would require adding 250,000 investment product customers (a 260% increase from the current 70,000), which was deemed unrealistic. The recommendation was to pursue cross-selling as a longer-term strategy while further investigating why the bank's competitive position in investments is only medium despite strong market growth (7%) and attractive ROE (14%).

## Discussion Questions

- Why is the bank's competitive position in the Investment segment only 'medium' despite offering similar products to competitors, and what specific barriers prevent higher penetration?
- What is a realistic timeline and phased approach for cross-selling investment products to achieve the $50M profit goal without overstretching sales and operations resources?
- Should the bank pursue alternative revenue growth strategies beyond cross-selling, such as expanding into higher-growth markets or introducing new products?
