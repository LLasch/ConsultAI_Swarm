# Case Brief: WOEM

**Company:** WOEM (Wedding cakes, clothing, and wedding planning)
**Industry:** retail
**Source:** Fuqua-Case-book-2014.pdf (pages 164–170)
**Tags:** profitability, revenue_diversification, wedding_industry, cost_analysis, market_entry, los_angeles, retail, business_portfolio

## Current Situation

WOEM operates two separate business entities: WOEM Cakes and WOEM Clothing. In 2013, WOEM Cakes sold 900 cakes at $1,000 average price with $600 average costs (down from 1,300 cakes in 2012). WOEM Clothing generated $150,000 in revenue but incurred $560,000 in costs, having been unprofitable since inception. The company serves two customer segments: middle-income individuals (40%) and wedding planners (60%). WOEM has industry knowledge and an existing network but no wedding planning experience.

## Key Challenges

WOEM, a Los Angeles-based wedding cake and clothing company, was unprofitable in 2013 due to declining cake sales (down 30% from 2012) and an unprofitable clothing division with spiraling costs. The CEO proposes launching a wedding planning service as a solution to achieve sustained profitability.
