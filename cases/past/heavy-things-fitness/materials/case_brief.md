**Company:** Heavy Things Fitness

**Tags:** market_segmentation, demographic_shift, customer_retention, fitness, decline

## Current Situation

Heavy Things Fitness was established during the fitness boom of the late 1970s and enjoyed decades of continual growth before recent decline. The company operates 15 locations in a mid-sized US metropolitan area (population 1-1.5M, comparable to Providence, Milwaukee, or Jacksonville). Heavy Things offers cardio, free weights, and weight machines with membership pricing and payment plans competitive with local rivals. The company has traditionally targeted serious fitness enthusiasts as their core demographic, believing them to be the most loyal customers.

## Key Challenges

Heavy Things Fitness, a 15-location fitness chain established in the late 1970s, has experienced alarming membership decline averaging 6% annually over the past 5 years despite the overall gym market growing at 3% annually. The company must determine the root causes of this attrition and develop a strategy to reverse the trend and return to growth.
