## Current Situation

Megabank is a credit card issuer that acquires new cardmembers through three channels: cross-selling to existing banking customers, direct mail campaigns to new customers, and private label partnerships with retailers and airlines. The company has identified the Hispanic market as a strategic growth opportunity. Current cross-sell volumes (5,000 per month) are significantly below industry norms (15,000-20,000 per month). The Hispanic population in the US is approximately 40 million, with 25 million eligible for credit cards (excluding those too young). Megabank currently has 2.5 million Hispanic cardmembers and aims to reach 7.5 million.

## Key Challenges

Megabank has significantly lower penetration in the Hispanic market compared to competing banks, despite this being a fast-growing demographic segment. The company needs to identify the root causes of underpenetration and develop a strategy to increase Hispanic market share from 10% to 30% over five years.
