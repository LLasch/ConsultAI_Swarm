# Case Brief: Apparel Retailer

**Company:** Premium brand apparel retailer (unnamed)
**Industry:** retail
**Source:** Haas_2006.pdf (pages 31–31)
**Tags:** market_share_decline, distribution_strategy, retail, premium_brand, channel_diversification, branding, competitive_analysis

---

## Current Situation

The company operates in the premium apparel retail segment competing against other premium brands and increasingly against mid-range substitutes. The overall market is growing steadily with no major consolidations. The company's marketing mix, pricing, and cost structure have remained largely unchanged. Distribution is concentrated in large multi-brand retail outlets (70%) with the remaining share through self-owned stores and smaller merchants (30%). Brand recognition among customers is low despite customers not being price sensitive.

## Key Challenges

A premium apparel retailer has experienced a significant decline in market share from 15% to 7% despite a growing overall market. The company needs to understand the root causes of this decline and develop a strategy to reverse the trend.

## Decision

The company should diversify its distribution channels by expanding self-owned stores and launching an online sales channel to reduce dependence on large retailers. Concurrently, the company must execute a significant branding program to increase customer pull and brand recognition across both existing and new channels.

## Outcome

Not disclosed

## Discussion Questions

- What is the primary driver of the company's market share loss—changes in customer preferences, competitor actions, or distribution channel dynamics?
- How should the company balance investment in direct distribution channels against potential channel conflict and brand positioning concerns?
- What branding and marketing initiatives would be most effective in building customer pull given the current low brand recognition?
