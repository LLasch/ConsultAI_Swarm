## Current Situation

Michigan Economic Development Corporation is a state agency tasked with economic development for the state of Michigan. The state was severely impacted by the 2009 economic recession, experiencing the highest unemployment rate in the nation and significant population loss as citizens relocated for work. Five years later, Michigan is slowly recovering but remains challenged. The state has allocated a $10MM special fund that MEDC can discretionarily deploy to incentivize business relocation and growth.

## Key Challenges

Michigan is recovering from the 2009 recession with the highest unemployment rate (13.4%) and net emigration (86,000+ citizens). MEDC needs to determine how to make Michigan more attractive to businesses and workers, with goals of adding 1,000 jobs and bringing in $40MM in capital expenditure within one year.
