## Current Situation

The year is 2014, following recovery from the 2008 financial collapse. The client believes market conditions are favorable for new market maker entry. Market makers earn fixed percentage commissions on transactions and provide liquidity. The four target markets have different characteristics in terms of transaction volume, total assets under management, competitive dynamics, and profitability profiles. One major player in equities recently experienced a rogue trader incident and is exiting.

## Key Challenges

A wealthy financier with finance experience but limited trading background is considering entering the capital markets as a market maker. He must decide which of four markets (Commodities, Options, Equities, Fixed Income) to enter with up to $2.5 billion to maximize NPV, given that he can only focus on one opportunity at a time.
