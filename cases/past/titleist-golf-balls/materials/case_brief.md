## Current Situation

Titleist is a global manufacturer and supplier of golf balls present in almost every market worldwide. Malawi is a small African country with a population of 11 million and GDP per capita of $158, with limited reliable demographic data. The golf market in Malawi includes 48,723 registered members with the Royal Malawi Golf Association and 206 golf courses. Currently, the market is served by three competitors: two local entrepreneurs importing from China and Callaway importing from the US, each holding approximately 1/3 market share.

## Key Challenges

Titleist is considering entering the Malawi golf ball market, where they currently have no presence. The CEO needs to determine whether market entry is viable and, if so, what the appropriate entry strategy should be given the small market size and existing competition.
