# Case Brief: Test Equipment Manufacturer

**Industry:** manufacturing

## Current Situation

The manufacturer produces custom test equipment ranging from $10,000 to $1 million by configuring products from standard subassemblies held in stock. The supply chain involves outsourced circuit board manufacturing (1-2 week cycle time), standard components from distributors (1-2 week lead time), and custom components from semiconductor manufacturers (9-month lead time). The company offers 26,000+ possible product configurations but experiences slow-moving inventory and occasional obsolescence when product versions change.

## Key Challenges

The client manufactures high-complexity, low-volume test equipment and currently holds 183 days of inventory—more than 4x the best-in-class benchmark of 39 days. The company needs to reduce inventory to 85 days or less while maintaining its 4-week customer delivery guarantee.

## Discussion Questions

- What are the root causes of excess inventory at each stage of the supply chain, and which have the greatest impact on overall days inventory outstanding?
- How can the company reduce the number of distinct subassemblies while maintaining product customization and customer choice?
- Should the company shift its inventory model from holding finished subassemblies to holding raw components, given its 4-week delivery commitment?
- What opportunities exist for outsourced manufacturers to procure standard components on behalf of the client, and how would this reduce inventory costs?
