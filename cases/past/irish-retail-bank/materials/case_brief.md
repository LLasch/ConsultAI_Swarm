## Current Situation

The bank is a decades-old institution that has maintained a stable 50% market share in Ireland alongside a similarly-sized competitor. Over the past decade, retail banking products have become increasingly complex and IT-intensive, requiring significant capital investment in technology infrastructure and hiring of IT-conversant staff. The bank owns its branch locations (low real estate costs) and has not conducted major redundancies despite labor law restrictions that make employee termination difficult. A strong union represents the majority of staff.

## Key Challenges

An Irish retail bank has suffered a decade of declining profitability despite maintaining constant 50% market share and revenues growing in line with GDP. The core issue is that operating costs have grown faster than revenues, driven primarily by IT infrastructure expansion, staff salary increases, and labor retention challenges.
