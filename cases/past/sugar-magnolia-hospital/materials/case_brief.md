## Current Situation

Sugar Magnolia is a large, full-service hospital in an urban area serving diverse patient segments through physician referrals. The hospital operates under three revenue models: fixed fees for service, cost-plus arrangements, and per diem charges. Key patient segments include obstetrics/labor (low profitability, declining volume), pediatrics (low profitability, declining volume), males 25-50 (medium profitability, stable volume), and elderly 65+ (high profitability, increasing volume). Fixed costs are $1,000 per patient with variable costs of $500 per patient-day, averaging 10-day stays across 1,000 annual patients.

## Key Challenges

Sugar Magnolia Hospital's profitability has declined significantly over five years to the point of financial crisis, threatening its ability to meet financial and social mission. The CEO seeks to understand the root causes of declining profitability and develop a turnaround strategy.
