## Current Situation

The Dome is a large tent-based recreational facility consisting of 17 themed zones, each sponsored by different companies with creative control over their exhibits. Opened in March 2000, the facility has experienced both operational challenges and reputational damage. Some zones are extremely popular while others attract minimal visitors. The facility has substantial fixed costs including maintenance, heating, lighting, staffing, and rent, with operating data showing maintenance requests increasing despite declining maintenance budgets.

## Key Challenges

The London Millennium Dome, a large recreational facility in Greenwich, is suffering from significant financial problems and negative publicity. The facility must be turned around within a 10-month window before closure, requiring a comprehensive strategy to improve financial performance.
