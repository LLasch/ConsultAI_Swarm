# Case Brief: Credit/Debit Card Processor

**Company:** Pierce Processing
**Industry:** finance
**Source:** Columbia_2006.pdf (pages 37–39)
**Tags:** pricing_strategy, revenue_diversification, market_entry, cannibalization, b2b, software_licensing, financial_services, market_segmentation

---

## Current Situation

Pierce Processing provides outsourced processing services to credit/debit card issuers, earning a percentage of transaction fees for credit authorization, fraud detection, and accounting services. The company currently serves 10 of the top 20 card issuers, which comprise 90% of the credit card market. The credit card transaction market grows at 3% annually, while Pierce has only 5% market share in the debit card segment, which is highly fragmented with no competitor exceeding 10% share.

## Key Challenges

Pierce Processing, a credit/debit card processor, has enjoyed 20 years of double-digit growth but needs to identify new strategies to sustain this growth rate. The company must evaluate whether to expand through new business models, market segments, and revenue streams while managing potential cannibalization risks.

## Decision

Not specified. The case presents multiple growth options for evaluation: (1) introducing a software licensing model in addition to outsourced processing, (2) gaining market share in the fragmented debit card segment through acquisition or partnership, and (3) entering the emerging store-value card market (projected 50% annual growth).

## Outcome

Not disclosed. The case provides quantitative frameworks for evaluation: software licensing model would generate $70M revenue over 5 years vs. $50M without it; store-value card segment entry would yield $300K first-year revenue with $100K accounting profit after depreciation, but does not specify which strategy Pierce ultimately chose or its actual results.

## Discussion Questions

- What steps could Pierce take to sustain its double-digit growth rate given the maturity of the credit card market?
- What are the risks and benefits of introducing a software licensing model alongside Pierce's existing outsourced processing service?
- Should Pierce prioritize gaining share in the fragmented debit card market or entering the higher-growth store-value card segment, and why?
- How should Pierce evaluate potential cannibalization effects when considering its new business model options?
