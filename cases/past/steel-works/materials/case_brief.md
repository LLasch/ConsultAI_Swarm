# Case Brief: Steel Works

**Company:** Steel Works
**Industry:** manufacturing
**Source:** Fuqua-Case-book-2014.pdf (pages 151–163)
**Tags:** cost_reduction, consolidation, manufacturing, capacity_planning, operations

---

## Current Situation

Steel Works is a global steel manufacturing company with multiple U.S. operations supplying aerospace, automotive, appliance, construction, and roofing industries. The company operates in a growing market with a 5-year CAGR of 2.5%. The newly hired CEO is open to technology investments if they reduce overall operating costs. The company operates three production units (A, B, C) with varying capacities and utilization rates, and manages shipping via both road and canal routes, including reliance on the Sparkling Water Canal which is undergoing a $50M revitalization project.

## Key Challenges

Steel Works, a major steel manufacturer based in Chicago, is facing intense price competition from rivals and needs to reduce operating costs. The company is considering closing one of its three production units to consolidate operations. The challenge is to identify which facility to shut down while maintaining production capacity and minimizing total costs.
