## Current Situation

The bank is a large Scottish retail bank that has operated in a stable market for several years with low competition. It has a similar product set to competitors and faces regulatory restrictions on mergers and takeovers. The bank has limited capital available for investment in new developments or initiatives. It currently generates revenue from margin spreads on borrowing and lending, fees, and investment income, with costs driven by staff, branches, infrastructure, and back-office operations.

## Key Challenges

A large Scottish retail bank is experiencing declining profitability in a stable, low-competition market. The bank must identify how to reverse this decline given limited funding for new investments and regulatory constraints that prevent mergers and takeovers.

## Discussion Questions

- Given the constraints of unionized labor, community resistance to branch closures, and inability to change margin spreads without triggering competitor reactions, what specific fee revenue opportunities should the bank prioritize to improve profitability?
- How can the bank structure its fee strategy (transaction fees, annual fees, and penalty fees) across different products (loans, debit accounts, credit cards) to increase revenue without alienating customers?
