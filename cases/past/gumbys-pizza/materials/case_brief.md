## Current Situation

Gumby's Pizza is a regional pizza chain with approximately 30 locations primarily in college towns throughout the South. The business is currently owned by retiring mom-and-pop operators. The chain generates roughly $125 million in annual revenues. Gumby's holds strong market position in the student segment (60% market share) and delivery channel (40% market share), but faces intense competition from national chains like Domino's and Papa Gino's.

## Key Challenges

A private equity firm is considering acquiring a 30-store mom-and-pop pizza chain in southern college towns with $125M in annual revenues. The PE firm needs to identify 2-3 feasible strategies to double gross margin within 3-5 years before selling the chain to another family owner.
