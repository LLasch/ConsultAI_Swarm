## Current Situation

XYZ Holiday Parks operates 4 medium-scale caravan parks (averaging 400 pitches each) located in Cornwall and Devon, operating seasonally from March to October. The parks serve British families seeking affordable holidays and feature comprehensive facilities including swimming pools, tennis courts, and restaurants. The company maintains strong customer satisfaction with 50% repeat visitor rates. Caravans cost £15,000-£40,000 with 15-year lifespans. Estimated annual revenue is approximately £30 million with 30% EBITDA margins.

## Key Challenges

A client is evaluating whether to acquire XYZ Holiday Parks, a operator of 4 static caravan parks in coastal UK locations. The assessment requires analyzing the business economics, competitive dynamics, and strategic considerations to determine if the acquisition represents a sound investment opportunity.
