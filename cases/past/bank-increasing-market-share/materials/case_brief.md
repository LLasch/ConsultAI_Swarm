## Current Situation

The bank operates in a competitive market with 5 major players offering diversified services across 4 business segments: Checking Accounts, Savings Accounts, Loans, and Mortgages. The bank holds the #2 position with 18% overall market share, close behind the leader at 20%. However, market share varies significantly by segment: Checking (21%), Business Accounts (20%), Loans (23%), and Mortgages (10%). Profitability per transaction also varies dramatically, from $5 for checking accounts to $1,000 for mortgages.

## Key Challenges

A profitable bank seeks to increase overall market share while maintaining profitability. The CEO is considering expanding the sales force but needs guidance on whether to invest and, if so, which sales force category (Branch Managers, Telemarketers, or Mortgage Agents) should receive additional headcount.

## Decision

The recommended decision is to increase mortgage agents. While telemarketers generate the highest total profitability (primarily from existing customers), mortgage agents are the only sales force category that drives significant new business acquisition. Since the CEO's explicit goal is market share growth and the bank has the lowest market share in mortgages (10%), investing in mortgage agents represents the best opportunity to gain new customers and expand market share.
