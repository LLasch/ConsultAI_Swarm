## Current Situation

The company operates in Commercial Risk Insurance in a single region with 4 million members (40% market share) and several direct competitors. Revenue is generated through premiums ($200/member/year) and costs come from claims paid to caregivers. The company is structured around functional groups (New Admits, Pre-authorizations, Case management) staffed with former field-based MDs and RNs now managing hundreds of healthcare professionals. Management lacks experience supervising large-scale remote organizations, having previously worked in small practices of 5-10 professionals.

## Key Challenges

A health insurance company swung from a $40M profit to a $40M loss year-over-year despite stable revenues. Per-member variable costs increased by $20/year, driven primarily by rising utilization of resources and inefficient management of healthcare providers, requiring identification of root causes and solutions.
