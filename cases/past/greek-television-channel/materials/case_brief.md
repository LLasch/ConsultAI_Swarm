# Case Brief: Greek Television Channel

**Company:** Greek free-to-air television channel
**Industry:** media
**Source:** LBS_2006.pdf (pages 114–125)
**Tags:** turnaround, media, advertising, greece, cost_structure, competitive_dynamics, profitability

---

## Current Situation

The channel was acquired three years ago but has consistently lost money. The free-to-air TV industry generates revenue primarily through advertising sales, with additional potential from product placement, content wholesale, TV shopping, audience-generated services, and merchandising. The channel's cost structure consists of programming costs (50%), broadcasting equipment and staff (30%), and overhead (20%). The competitive landscape is fragmented with multiple channels competing for the same advertising budget.

## Key Challenges

A privately owned free-to-air TV channel in Greece has been unprofitable for three years with an EBIT margin of -30%. The chairman and sole shareholder must decide whether the business can be turned around and, if so, what specific steps should be taken to achieve profitability.
