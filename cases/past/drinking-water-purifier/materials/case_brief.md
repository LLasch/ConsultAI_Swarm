# Case Brief: Drinking Water Purifier

**Company:** A leading global consumer goods company
**Industry:** consumer goods

**Tags:** market_entry, india, consumer_goods, estimation, emerging_markets, product_launch, developing_countries

## Current Situation

The company is a leading global consumer goods manufacturer that has successfully launched a water purification product (powder sachet that purifies 10 liters of water) in multiple developing countries with varying success rates. The company already has an existing presence in India selling other products. India presents a large potential market with significant portions of the population lacking access to clean drinking water infrastructure, particularly in rural areas where 70% of the population resides.

## Key Challenges

A global consumer goods company has developed a water purification sachet product and wants to determine whether to enter the Indian market with this new offering and, if so, how to do so. The company needs to assess market opportunity, customer needs, competitive dynamics, and optimal entry strategy.
