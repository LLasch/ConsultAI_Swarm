## Current Situation

Terrific Tennis plc is the UK's leading owner/operator of fitness centers, offering comprehensive facilities including gyms, pools, spas, saunas, tennis courts, and childcare services in suburban locations with parking. The company has achieved significant success in the UK market and is now evaluating international expansion opportunities. Initial research suggests a pure tennis court facility may be the optimal entry strategy for the German market, with estimated annual operating costs of GBP 147,000 for a 10-court facility.

## Key Challenges

Terrific Tennis plc, the UK's leading fitness center operator, is considering expansion into Continental Europe, specifically Germany. The company must determine whether to enter the German market with its full-service fitness center model or a specialized tennis court facility, and at what price point.
