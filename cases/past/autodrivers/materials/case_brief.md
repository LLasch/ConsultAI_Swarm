## Current Situation

AutoDrivers is a mid-size auto insurance company operating primarily in NJ, PA, and MD with 2013 revenues of $300M. The company currently offers only auto insurance in a highly competitive market where major competitors are all exploring telematics options. The US auto insurance industry generated $3B in revenue in 2013. Insurance is a commoditized business, and companies are differentiating through customer engagement services such as safe driver competitions and teen-driving programs.

## Key Challenges

AutoDrivers, a medium-sized auto insurance company, is experiencing declining market share (5% annual decline over 3 years) and needs to decide between implementing OBD (On-Board Diagnostics) or a mobile app telematics solution to attract new customers and increase market share. The company must also determine how to increase customer engagement and expand in low brand perception states.
