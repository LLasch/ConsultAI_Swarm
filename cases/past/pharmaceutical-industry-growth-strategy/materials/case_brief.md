## Current Situation

The company operates in two therapy areas: a mature, traditional core business (generating twice the revenue but equal profit) sold through general practitioners with 6-8 years of IP protection, and a high-growth niche business with unique, highly profitable products sold to specialists and hospitals. The pharmaceutical industry is R&D-driven with long development cycles from research to product launch. The company is highly profitable and has sufficient capital for investment, making the 12-15% growth target realistic given industry dynamics.

## Key Challenges

A profitable pharmaceutical company with a world-leading position in one therapy area is currently growing at 8% annually but has a CEO target of 12-15% growth (to triple revenue in 10 years). The company's R&D pipeline is thin and the R&D department is significantly underspending against its 16% of revenue budget allocation, with senior management lacking visibility into R&D operations.

## Discussion Questions

- What are the underlying root causes of R&D underspending and the thin pipeline—is it insufficient good research projects, lack of talent, motivation issues, or budget control problems?
- Should the company pursue geographical expansion, increased market share in existing markets, or development of new products in non-related therapy areas to achieve its growth targets?
- How should senior management improve visibility and control over R&D operations, and what organizational and management control procedures are needed?
