**Industry:** healthcare

## Current Situation

The client is a leading medical device company that recently completed an acquisition to grow its orthopedics division. Post-acquisition quality audits revealed significant manufacturing problems: 5 affected sites with 25 quality defects each (125 total defects). The company currently has resources capable of remediating defects at a rate of 0.125 defects per day. The FDA compliance deadline is non-negotiable at 6 months from the start date. At current rates, completion would require approximately 33.3 months, making the goal unachievable without intervention.

## Key Challenges

A medical device company has acquired a smaller orthopedics firm and discovered 125 quality defects across 5 manufacturing sites that must be remediated within 6 months to avoid FDA violations. At the current remediation rate of 0.125 defects per day, the company cannot meet the deadline and needs to develop a feasible plan to complete all remediation efforts on time.

## Discussion Questions

- Will the client meet its 6-month remediation goals with current resources, and by what factor does capacity need to increase?
- What are the viable options to accelerate remediation (e.g., hire additional staff, increase productivity, outsource work, prioritize defects), and what are the trade-offs in cost, feasibility, and risk for each option?
- How would you prioritize and sequence the remediation efforts across the 5 sites to maximize efficiency and manage execution risk?
