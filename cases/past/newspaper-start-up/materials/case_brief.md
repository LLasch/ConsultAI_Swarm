## Current Situation

This is a teaching case from the Haas Consulting Club Case Book designed for consultant training. The scenario involves evaluating a business plan at an entrepreneur's forum. The startup has no external capital and severe resource constraints, with only 6 carriers available to deliver newspapers across multiple square-kilometer zones. The newspaper must be printed by 4AM and delivered by 6AM, providing a narrow 2-hour delivery window.

## Key Challenges

A proposed business plan calls for door-to-door distribution of an online newspaper in Cologne, Germany using only 6 bicycle-riding newspaper carriers covering the downtown area. The core challenge is determining whether the distribution plan is operationally feasible given the time constraints and resource limitations.

## Decision

Not specified

## Outcome

The quantitative analysis reveals the plan is infeasible: with homes averaging 10 meters apart, each square kilometer contains 10,000 stops. With only 7,200 seconds available (2 hours), carriers would need to deliver more than one newspaper per second—a practical impossibility. Two alternatives are proposed: reducing the distribution area to focus on profitable subscribers, or changing the distribution method through co-distribution partnerships or alternative channels.

## Discussion Questions

- How would you evaluate the feasibility of the proposed distribution plan without detailed operational knowledge?
- What key assumptions must you make to assess whether 6 bicycle carriers can deliver newspapers across downtown Cologne in the required timeframe?
- Given the budget constraints preventing additional hiring, what alternative distribution strategies could make the business model viable?
