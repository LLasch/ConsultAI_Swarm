## Current Situation

The client is a professional services firm that originated in corporate tax preparation and audit services (75% of revenue) and expanded into wealth advisory services for high-net-worth individuals. The firm differentiates itself through independence and objectivity, unlike traditional competitors such as investment banks and private banks. The wealth advisory market grew significantly due to wealth creation in the mid-to-late 1990s, attracting new entrants including investment banks, mutual fund companies, and online players. However, the firm's ability to control the value chain is constrained by SEC regulations prohibiting transaction execution.

## Key Challenges

A wealth advisory services firm with $400M in revenue is facing aggressive competition from large diversified financial services firms and a well-funded pure-play internet competitor (myCFO.com). The firm has lost its top five partners to the new competitor and is experiencing declining revenues in certain service lines, requiring a strategic response to defend market position.
