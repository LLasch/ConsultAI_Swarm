**Company:** Orange Yoga Studio
**Industry:** wellness

## Current Situation

Orange Yoga Studio is an upscale yoga studio in New York City that has been operating successfully for 5 years. The studio offers Vinyasa Flow classes (5 per weekday, 4 per weekend) with high-quality instruction and a relaxed atmosphere. Yoga Jones recently invested in major upgrades including hardwood floors, fresh paint, and new shower facilities, financed primarily through loans. The competitive landscape for yoga studios in NYC has remained stable over the past three years.

## Key Challenges

Yoga Jones, owner of Orange Yoga Studio in NYC, has noticed declining profits over the past year despite the studio's popularity and high-quality instruction. She lacks detailed financial records and has made significant capital investments financed through loans, making it unclear whether she is actually losing money and how to address the financial decline.

## Decision

Not specified

## Outcome

Financial analysis reveals Orange Yoga is operating at a monthly loss of approximately $1,650. Monthly revenue is $12,864 (from $12 per-class fees with 8 students on weekdays and 6 on weekends), while monthly costs total $14,450 (including $3,500 rent, $150 insurance, $3,200 administration, $7,100 instructor salaries, and $500 loan payments). The profit decline resulted from a $2,000 monthly rent increase and $10 per-class salary increase for instructors.

## Discussion Questions

- How should Yoga Jones balance her commitment to fair teacher compensation and studio location with the financial reality of the business losses?
- What are the viable options to improve profitability without sacrificing the studio's upscale positioning and quality instruction?
