## Current Situation

The Department of Transportation operates six transportation modes including ferries. The ferry system operates 24 vessels across 18 terminals, serving over 20 million passengers annually, including both commuters and tourists. The total annual ferry budget is $270 million, with costs distributed across diesel fuel (33%), salaries and wages (40%), overhead (20%), and miscellaneous expenses (7%). The system employs approximately 2,000 employees with average salaries of $54,000.

## Key Challenges

A state Department of Transportation must improve the financial performance of its ferry system to meet budget cuts and rising energy costs. The client needs to cut $70M from its annual ferry budget within 2 years while increasing profit margins.
