# Case Brief: Candy UK – All Liquorice Acquisition of Black Delight

**Company:** All Liquorice (AL)
**Industry:** manufacturing
**Source:** LBS_2006.pdf (pages 75–81)
**Tags:** acquisition, valuation, synergies, manufacturing, consolidation, competitive_strategy, cost_reduction, market_share

## Current Situation

The UK candy industry (chocolate, liquorice, chewing gum) has consolidated significantly with four main competitors: Chewin (market leader, most profitable), All Liquorice, Black Delight, and Devotion. All Liquorice has £500k in sales with 6% net margin and £330k market cap. Black Delight has £200k in sales with 5% net margin and £100k market cap. The competitive landscape and market consolidation create both strategic opportunities and defensive pressures.

## Key Challenges

All Liquorice is considering acquiring competitor Black Delight in a consolidated candy market dominated by four players. The board must determine whether the acquisition is strategically sound and at what price to bid.
