## Current Situation

The client is the largest company in its industry, operating in a three-tier distribution model: manufacturers → client → resellers → end consumers. The reseller market segments into Corporate (largest), Value-Added (medium), and E-tailer (smallest). From 2000 to 2002, the overall industry experienced significant contraction in both price and volume, with the client's market share declining from approximately 33% to 25%. Industry prices fell across all segments, and volume declined substantially.

## Key Challenges

A technology product warehouser that distributes telephony technologies through resellers has experienced eight consecutive quarters of declining profit. The company must diagnose the root causes of this decline and develop recommendations to reverse the negative trend.
