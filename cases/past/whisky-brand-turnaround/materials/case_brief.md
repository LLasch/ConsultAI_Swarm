# Case Brief: Whisky Brand Turnaround

**Company:** Unnamed whisky company
**Industry:** manufacturing
**Source:** Wharton_2010.pdf (pages 54–57)
**Tags:** pricing, turnaround, profit_optimization, elasticity, competitive_response, brand_positioning

---

## Current Situation

The company is a diversified liquor manufacturer with an existing whisky brand in the market. Current pricing is $15.00 per bottle with sales of 10m units annually. Production costs are 75% variable and 25% fixed. Market research suggests demand elasticity at the proposed higher price point.

## Key Challenges

A whisky brand is considering a price increase from $15.00 to $20.00 per bottle. The company must evaluate whether this pricing strategy will improve profitability despite an expected reduction in unit sales from 10m to 8m bottles.

## Decision

The company decided to increase the price to $20.00 per bottle based on financial analysis showing superior profit potential despite lower volume.

## Outcome

The price increase is projected to generate an additional $22m in profits, representing a ~30% increase in total profitability compared to the current $15.00 pricing.

## Discussion Questions

- What complications might arise from such a significant price increase in the whisky market?
- How should the company prepare for and mitigate competitive responses to this pricing move?
