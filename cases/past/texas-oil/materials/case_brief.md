## Current Situation

Texas Oil is a major integrated oil and gas company competing with ExxonMobil, Shell, and Chevron, with assets in 72 countries across 5 continents and one-quarter of the US oil market share. The company recently purchased a large tract of land in West Texas from an independent oil company. All purchased land has proven oil and gas reserves with 90% confidence. However, the company is capital constrained due to low commodity prices in the global oil and gas market and significant cost overruns on a refinery project in Angola. The 320,000-acre lease will expire in 3 years, creating time pressure for development decisions.

## Key Challenges

Texas Oil, a large integrated oil and gas company, has acquired 320,000 acres of land with proven reserves in West Texas but faces a capital constraint due to low crude oil prices and an over-budget Angola refinery. The company must determine which portions of the newly acquired land to develop within a 3-year lease window, as only 60% of the purchased acreage can be developed before lease expiration.
