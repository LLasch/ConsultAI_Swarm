## Current Situation

This is a teaching case designed to illustrate basic profit analysis in professional services. Law firms hire associates directly from law school at a fixed starting salary. The case assumes the firm has available work to assign to new hires. Associates bill their time at hourly rates to clients.

## Key Challenges

A law firm must evaluate whether hiring new associates at $100,000 annually is economically viable. The core question is whether the marginal revenue generated by a new lawyer exceeds the total cost of employment, including salary, benefits, overhead, training, and recruiting expenses.
