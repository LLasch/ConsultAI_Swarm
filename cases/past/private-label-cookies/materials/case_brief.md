# Case Brief: Private-Label Cookies

**Company:** US-based branded cookie manufacturer
**Industry:** manufacturing
**Source:** Haas_2006.pdf (pages 46–49)
**Tags:** private_label, market_entry, competitive_threat, pricing, manufacturing, branded_vs_private_label, market_share, cost_structure

---

## Current Situation

The cookie manufacturer is a major branded player in a relatively flat $1B US market dominated by grocery outlets (90% of sales) and mass merchandisers (10%). Five years ago, private-label cookies emerged and have grown steadily at the expense of branded sales. The client's main competitor began supplying private-label cookies 2.5 years ago and now holds 40% of that segment. Meanwhile, the client has actually increased its share of the branded segment from 60% to 70% over five years, but overall sales have declined due to market share losses to private-label.

## Key Challenges

A US-based cookie manufacturer faces growing competition from private-label cookies, which have increased from 10% to 20% of the market over 2.5 years. The manufacturer must decide whether to enter the private-label segment to compete with rivals who are already supplying private-label products to retailers, while the overall market remains flat and the client's sales have declined from $600M to $560M.
