## Current Situation

AluCo Closures is a subsidiary of AluCo, one of the world's largest aluminium producers. The company manufactures two alloy series (3-series for water/carbonated drinks, 8-series for spirits) used in bottle closures. The market is valued at 40,000 tons annually at approximately €1,000/ton, growing at 3% per year. AluCo Closures has historically maintained the highest product quality and 65% market share in both segments. The customer base consists of fragmented, competitive closure manufacturers who face significant price pressure from large multinational drinks companies like Diageo and Nestle.

## Key Challenges

AluCo Closures, a dominant aluminium alloy producer for bottle closures with 65% market share, is experiencing declining revenue despite a growing market. The company needs to identify the root causes of market share loss and develop a strategy to address competitive threats and changing customer dynamics.
