# Case Brief: Italian Paper

**Company:** Italian Paper Manufacturer
**Industry:** manufacturing
**Source:** Columbia_2006.pdf (pages 16–18)
**Tags:** capacity_expansion, market_entry, growth_strategy, europe, competitive_analysis

---

## Current Situation

The company is a high-end glossy and matte paper manufacturer serving premium segments (corporate brochures, luxury brand advertising). It operates one factory producing 150,000 tons annually with 90% sales in Italy (20% market share) and 10% international sales. The Italian market is growing at 10% annually, while European markets like Germany are growing at 15%. The competitive landscape includes a dominant Italian competitor with 40% share, one international player with 10%, and fragmented smaller players holding 30%.

## Key Challenges

An Italian high-end paper manufacturer must decide whether to invest $100 million to build a second factory with 150,000 tons annual capacity. The company currently dominates the Italian market (20% share) but sells only 10% of output internationally, and needs to determine if additional capacity can be profitably deployed.

## Discussion Questions

- Does the Italian market have sufficient demand to absorb an additional 150,000 tons of capacity, or should the company focus on international expansion?
- Given that no competitors are investing in additional capacity, what are the reasons—lack of opportunity or resource constraints—and what does this signal about market conditions?
- What are the relative merits of building a new factory versus expanding existing capacity or acquiring a competitor to achieve growth objectives?
