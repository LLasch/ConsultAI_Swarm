## Current Situation

The case is set in 2005 and examines a growing trend in the Consumer Packaged Goods industry where large retailers request manufacturers to produce private label versions of their products. The client is an established bottled water company with an existing brand carried by major retailers. The retailer in question is a powerful merchant with significant shelf space and purchasing power, making the relationship strategically important.

## Key Challenges

A major retailer (e.g. Walmart) is requesting that a bottled water manufacturer produce a private label version of its product at a lower price point under the retailer's brand. The company must evaluate whether accepting this opportunity will create sufficient value to offset potential risks including cannibalization of its own brand and operational complexity.
