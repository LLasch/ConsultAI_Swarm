## Current Situation

Prime Inc. is part of a large media conglomerate (4-star media) operating in online and print media across three segments: Enthusiast Media, Consumer Guides, and Business News. The business news segment operates both a print magazine and a website that primarily reproduces print content. Market research reveals that subscription rates are waning, with low brand awareness among non-subscribers and declining perceived insight among current and former readers. The average reader age has increased by 10 years over 5 years, and Prime Inc. has the lowest demographic share and highest ad pricing among top 5 business news magazines.

## Key Challenges

Prime Inc., a business news magazine segment, has experienced declining revenues over the past 5 years after decades of stable growth. The case requires determining the root causes of revenue decline and recommending strategies to stimulate growth in a competitive media landscape.

## Discussion Questions

- What internal, external, and customer factors have contributed to Prime Inc.'s revenue decline?
