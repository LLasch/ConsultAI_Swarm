## Current Situation

The case involves a national premium supermarket chain (comparable to Waitrose in the UK) with a mid-size suburban store located between a small convenience store and a large out-of-town format. The store is achieving sales and profits as good as any other location in the chain, which is counterintuitive given the local demographics are relatively poor and the chain focuses on high-quality groceries at premium prices. A one-week planned refurbishment provides a rare opportunity to reconfigure store layout and product assortment without extended closure.

## Key Challenges

A UK supermarket chain is refurbishing a mid-size suburban store and must decide what changes, if any, to make to the types of goods sold. The store is currently outperforming other chain locations despite being located in a relatively poor suburb and selling premium products, creating a strategic puzzle about how to optimize the store's product mix.
