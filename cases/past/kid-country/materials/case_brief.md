## Current Situation

Kid Country built a substantial business around hybrid rap/rock/country music during an early market boom, expanding into multiple product categories including apparel, beverages, music sales, and concert tickets. However, the core music genre has become increasingly unpopular, and accountants have flagged consistent revenue declines across the business. The company maintains diversified revenue streams but faces profitability challenges across different business units.

## Key Challenges

Kid Country, a music entertainment company with diversified product lines (concert tickets, apparel, beverages, CD/MP3 sales), is experiencing declining revenues over three years. The company needs to identify which business lines are performing well and which require strategic intervention or exit.
