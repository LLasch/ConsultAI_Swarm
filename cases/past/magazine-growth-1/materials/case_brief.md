# Case Brief: Magazine Growth

**Company:** Prime Inc. (4-Star Media)
**Industry:** media
**Source:** Columbia_2006.pdf (pages 76–79)
**Tags:** media, advertising, pricing_strategy, market_research, digital_disruption, content_strategy, competitive_positioning, revenue_decline, target_demographics, magazine

---

## Current Situation

4-Star Media is a large media conglomerate operating in online and print media across three segments: enthusiast media, consumer guides, and business news. Prime Inc. is the business news division, comprising both print magazine and a website that reproduces magazine content. Advertising accounts for approximately 75% of revenues compared to subscriptions (3:1 ratio). Market research revealed low brand awareness among non-subscribers, perceived lack of insight among current/lapsed readers, and reader base aging 10 years over 5 years.

## Key Challenges

Prime Inc., a business news magazine segment within 4-Star Media, has experienced declining revenues over the past 5 years after decades of stable growth. The case requires determining the root causes of the decline and recommending strategies to stimulate growth in both print and online channels.

## Decision

The company should lower advertising prices to be competitive (especially considering Prime Inc. had the highest price per ad per 1000 readers among top 5 competitors despite lowest share of target demographic), implement differentiated pricing based on advertiser volume and price sensitivity, refresh content to appeal to younger readers with human-interest and technology-focused stories, and pursue alternative growth strategies including leveraging the website, cross-promoting through other organizational media, developing proprietary content, and launching advertising campaigns.

## Outcome

Not disclosed

## Discussion Questions

- How should Prime Inc. balance the need to attract younger readers with the risk of alienating its existing older subscriber base?
- Given that advertising revenue is 3x subscription revenue, should Prime Inc. focus its turnaround efforts primarily on subscriber acquisition or advertiser retention and acquisition?
