## Current Situation

Meditest is a European medical equipment manufacturer specializing in blood sample testing machines for diabetes monitoring, priced above $20,000. Their core market serves professional lab operators. The company has designed a new product line—glucore—a small, portable, easy-to-use device for patient self-monitoring that requires minimal consumables. This represents a strategic shift toward the consumer market segment to achieve higher growth rates beyond their stagnant professional equipment business.

## Key Challenges

Meditest manufactures expensive professional blood glucose testing equipment for labs, but this market is growing slowly. The company has developed a new consumer-oriented portable glucose monitoring device called glucore and must evaluate whether to launch it in the UK market and what factors to consider.

Not specified

## Outcome

Market analysis projects 108,000 annual purchasers in the UK (10% of 1.08M diabetics), growing to 144,000 people with diabetes in 4 years. At projected 25% market share and falling price point (from £120 to £60), revenues would reach £2.16 million annually in 4 years. However, this revenue was deemed insufficient for company growth objectives, suggesting the need to expand geographically or adjust pricing/market positioning strategy.

## Discussion Questions

- What are the key levers to increase revenue potential beyond the £2.16M projection for the UK market alone?
- How should Meditest price glucore considering both consumer value (convenience, time savings) and insurance company willingness to pay for preventive monitoring devices?
- Should Meditest pursue geographic expansion to other European markets, or develop additional value-added services for their core professional equipment business instead?
