## Current Situation

PharmaCo. has developed Drug X, a new AML (acute myeloid leukemia) cancer treatment that shows tremendous potential in preliminary Phase I trials. The company has no prior experience taking a drug to market and must navigate the lengthy FDA approval process (Phase I, II, and III trials). The drug has a 10-year patent life and would be priced at approximately $125,000 per patient annually. The U.S. market represents 3,300 annual AML patients, with doctors indicating willingness to prescribe the drug to 70% of patients. Competition is expected to enter the market after Year 4, capturing 25% market share.

## Key Challenges

PharmaCo. has developed a promising new AML cancer drug (Drug X) but faces extremely high costs to obtain FDA approval and bring it to market. A private equity firm has offered $150M for 50% of all future drug profits, and the company must decide whether to accept this partnership, self-fund development, or pursue an alternative strategy.
