**Industry:** healthcare

## Current Situation

Happy Healthcare operates 10 hospitals in the UK (compared to NHS's 1000+), typically half the size of NHS hospitals and without emergency departments. In 2005, the company generated £100m in revenue with only £0.5m operating profit, representing less than 1% of the hospital market. Revenue comes from patients with private medical insurance or paying out-of-pocket. The private hospital sector comprises about 10% of the UK healthcare market, while the NHS dominates. Happy Healthcare seeks to improve operational efficiency and is evaluating strategic growth opportunities.

## Key Challenges

Happy Healthcare, a small UK private hospital operator with 10 hospitals and less than 1% market share, faces intense competition from the dominant NHS and other private healthcare groups. The company must decide whether to accept bulk NHS contracts to reduce public waiting lists, while considering implications for capacity, profitability, and competitive differentiation.
