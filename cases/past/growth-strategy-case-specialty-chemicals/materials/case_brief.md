# Case Brief: Growth Strategy Case (Specialty Chemicals)

**Company:** Performance Chemicals division of a $7 billion Specialty Chemicals Company
**Industry:** manufacturing
**Source:** Haas_2006.pdf (pages 23–24)
**Tags:** growth_strategy, specialty_chemicals, manufacturing, business_model_innovation, market_analysis, product_service_bundling, commoditization, organizational_change

---

## Current Situation

Performance Chemicals is a division of a $7 billion specialty chemicals company that manufactures products including asphalt additives, sodium chemicals, and paper pulp bleaching solutions. All six businesses within the division have grown at 3-4% for the last five years, matching overall market growth rates. The company has historically operated with a product-focused, volume-driven mentality ("make it by the ton and sell it by the carload"). Products face limited intellectual property protection and are threatened by global competition and commoditization pressures.

## Key Challenges

The Performance Chemicals division, a $250 million business unit, has been growing at only 3-4% annually despite the parent company's CEO setting an ambitious 10-15% growth target. The division manager is skeptical about untapped market potential and seeks strategic guidance on how to accelerate growth in a commoditized, competitive industry.

## Decision

Deloitte Consulting conducted a growth diagnostic and 2-day workshops with each business unit to develop alternative strategies using the Valuable Formula methodology. Recommended strategies included product-service bundling, solutions-based selling, vendor-managed inventory systems, becoming a one-stop shop for specialty chemicals, outsourcing, and entering new markets for existing technological capabilities. The client agreed with recommendations and implemented a subset of the strategies.

## Outcome

Preliminary projections indicated the potential to quadruple the size of the Performance Chemicals group from $250 million to $1 billion in 5 years, representing approximately 20% annual growth. The client agreed with Deloitte's recommendations and began implementation.

## Discussion Questions

- How would you assess whether the Performance Chemicals division can realistically achieve 10%+ annual growth given current market conditions and competitive dynamics?
- What are the key cultural and operational changes required to shift from a product-centric to a solutions-based business model?
- Which growth strategies—product-service bundling, vendor-managed inventory, market expansion, or becoming a one-stop-shop—are most feasible and likely to generate sustainable competitive advantage?
