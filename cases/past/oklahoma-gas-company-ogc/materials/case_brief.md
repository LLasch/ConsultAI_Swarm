## Current Situation

Oklahoma Gas Company is a well-capitalized, medium-sized private energy company with a long history of drilling and producing natural gas wells in Oklahoma. The company owns 100% of mineral rights on its diverse land assets. Profitability has declined over recent years due to natural gas prices dropping to five-year lows, partly caused by industry-wide oversupply resulting from companies like OGC perfecting unconventional drilling techniques. Annual demand for natural gas has remained stable despite seasonal fluctuations.

## Key Challenges

OGC faces declining profitability due to oversupply and low natural gas prices, with profits projected to turn negative next year. The company must decide whether to accept a $250M acquisition offer from a larger competitor or pursue alternative strategic options to create shareholder value.
