## Current Situation

Pacific Northwest Telco is a regional telecom provider offering cable television, fixed telephone lines, internet, and mobile phone services. The company currently operates across four service lines with stable profitability and revenue metrics. However, new market entrants and increased competition from national carriers are creating pressure to grow and stabilize the customer base. The company operates in a consolidating industry with significant M&A and product line expansion activity.

## Key Challenges

A large regional telecommunications provider faces increased competition from major carriers like Verizon and AT&T, threatening its customer base despite currently stable profitability and revenue. The client needs to identify strategies to increase customer relationships and revenue to maintain market position.
