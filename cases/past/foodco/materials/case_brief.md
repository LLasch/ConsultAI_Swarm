# Case Brief: FoodCo

**Company:** FoodCo
**Industry:** food
**Source:** Wharton_2010.pdf (pages 82–85)
**Tags:** cost_reduction, labor_efficiency, operations, capital_constraints, profitability_enhancement, manufacturing

---

## Current Situation

FoodCo produces ethnic food sold in cups (e.g. cup noodles) as its sole revenue stream. Current revenues are $19M with costs totaling $16M after COGS of $3M. The company is operating at full capacity with unmet demand but is capital constrained and cannot build new operations centers. Cost structure breaks down into: Labor (50%), Equipment (25%), Administrative (20%), and Other (5%).

## Key Challenges

FoodCo, an ethnic food manufacturer, needs to improve profitability while facing capital constraints that prevent expansion through new production facilities. The company must identify efficiency gains and cost reduction opportunities to enhance margins without significant capital investment.

## Decision

Invest $500,000 in training and equipment reconfiguration to reduce repair time from 22% to 5%, which will improve labor efficiency and enable higher production volumes without new facilities.

## Outcome

The efficiency improvement generates 17% cost savings on the $8M labor cost base, totaling $1.36M in annual savings. Revenue grows from $19M to $22.23M (17% increase). After the $500K investment, profitability reaches approximately $7M, though this still falls short of the two-year target and requires additional growth initiatives.

## Discussion Questions

- What framework should be used to approach profitability enhancement when capital expansion is not feasible?
- How can efficiency savings in labor translate to revenue growth, and what is the return on the $500K equipment investment?
