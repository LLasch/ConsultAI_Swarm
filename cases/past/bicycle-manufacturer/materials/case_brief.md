## Current Situation

The company manufactures three categories of bicycles: racing bikes (high-end, 33% margin), mainstream bikes (mid-range, 23% margin), and children's bikes (entry-level, 20% margin). Five years ago, the company had a commanding 60% market share in the flat racing segment ($300MM market). Over the past five years, profits declined from $60MM to $44MM as the company expanded into mainstream and children's segments while losing market share in its core racing business.

## Key Challenges

A 25-year-old bicycle manufacturer has experienced declining profits over the past five years despite expanding into new product segments. The company needs to understand what is driving the profit decline and develop recommendations to reverse the trend.
