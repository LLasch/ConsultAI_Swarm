# Case Brief: The Coffee Grind

**Company:** Coffee Grind Division (unnamed parent company)
**Industry:** manufacturing
**Source:** Wharton_2010.pdf (pages 74–77)
**Tags:** profitability, distribution_channel, market_positioning, commoditization, mature_product

---

## Current Situation

The division manufactures automatic drip coffeemakers (4-cup, 10-cup, and 12-cup models) for the US and Canadian markets. It has operated with minimal changes over 20 years with relatively constant production volumes and a consistent product mix. The Michigan facility operates at 90-95% capacity with mature, efficient production lines. The company holds a mid-price market position with several competitors at similar price points.

## Key Challenges

A coffeemaker division that has been profitable for 20 years is experiencing declining profits despite steady volumes and healthy historical margins. The CEO seeks to understand the underlying causes of this profit decline and requests broader strategic insights beyond basic profitability analysis.
