# CASE BRIEF: Boeing 737 MAX 8 Disasters

## 1. Client Overview

**Boeing Company** - Major aerospace manufacturer and defense contractor
- **Founded:** 1916 by timber baron William E. Boeing
- **Core Business:** Commercial aircraft, defense systems, space technology
- **Mission/Legacy:** Historically "world-class" company known for building aircraft "better, faster, and cheaper than anyone else"
- **Market Position:** By 1990, held 62% of commercial airline market vs. McDonnell Douglas (23%) and Airbus (15%)
- **Culture:** Heritage of engineering excellence, "Boeing family" atmosphere with lifelong employment and promotion from within

## 2. Current Situation

**Financial/Operational Impact:**
- 737 MAX grounded for **21 months** (March 2019 - November 2020)
- **1,043 total order losses** through October 2020 (595 removed + 448 canceled)
- Remaining backlog: **3,320 MAX orders** vs. competitor Airbus A320neo: **5,956 orders**
- **346 total fatalities** across two crashes (189 + 157)
- Return to service authorized November 18, 2020, amid COVID-19 pandemic impact

**Market Context:**
- Commercial aviation safety at record levels: 1 death per 100 million passenger miles (2018)
- Global air travel growth: 2.7B to 4.5B passengers (2010-2019)
- Complex industry ecosystem: 1,400+ airlines, 26,000 aircraft, 360,000 pilots

## 3. Key Challenges

1. **MCAS System Design Flaws:** Maneuvering Characteristics Augmentation System caused uncommanded nose-down movement based on erroneous angle-of-attack data

2. **Regulatory Oversight Failures:** FAA certification process had "communication gaps," "management weaknesses," and inadequate independence per DOT Inspector General

3. **Cultural Transformation Issues:** Shift from engineering-focused "Heritage Boeing" culture to financial/schedule-driven approach post-McDonnell Douglas merger

4. **Stakeholder Trust Crisis:** Airlines, pilots, and regulators worldwide lost confidence; "largest and most respected airlines were screaming that Boeing had hidden...deadly software"

5. **Competitive Market Position:** Lost significant market share to Airbus during grounding period

6. **Crisis Communication Failures:** Initial response blamed airline maintenance and pilot error rather than acknowledging system issues

## 4. Stakeholder Perspectives

**Boeing Leadership:**
- CEO Dennis Muilenburg: "...the 737 MAX is safe" (repeated declarations)
- VP Mike Sinnett: Promised fix in "not a year, but...maybe six weeks-ish" to "tame the system"

**Government Officials:**
- President Trump: Ordered grounding March 13, 2019 under pressure
- Congressman Sam Graves: "Pilots trained in the United States would have been able to handle the emergencies on both jets"

**Industry Insiders:**
- Investigative reporter Peter Robison: "Behind the scenes, some of the largest and most respected airlines in the world were screaming that Boeing had hidden the existence of potentially deadly software inside their planes"

**Heritage Boeing Employees:**
- Former executive on T Wilson era: "He ran the company. It did not run him"
- Engineers described "Boeing family" atmosphere with "deep commitment to quality engineering and pride in the work"

## 5. Financial Highlights

**Historical Performance:**
- 1990: $20.3B sales, $973M earnings, 62% market share
- 737 became world's best-selling jetliner by 1990

**Current Impact (specific numbers limited in materials):**
- Major revenue loss during 21-month grounding

- Competitive disadvantage: 3,320 vs. 5,956 backlog ratio vs. Airbus

**Industry Context:**
- U.S. aviation: 1 death per 100M passenger miles
- Global growth: 3.1T to 5.4T passenger miles (2010-2019)
