# Case Brief: PayPal_FinancialWellness

# CASE BRIEF: PayPal Financial Wellness Initiative

## 1. Client Overview

**PayPal Holdings, Inc.**
- **Business**: Digital payments company operating two-sided network connecting consumers and merchants
- **Mission**: "Democratize financial services to ensure that everyone, regardless of background or economic standing, had access to affordable, convenient, and secure products and services to take control of their financial lives"
- **Leadership**: Dan Schulman, President and CEO since 2015
- **Strategic Vision**: Using technology to "turn the paradigm that it's expensive to be poor upside down"

## 2. Current Situation

**Market Position & Growth:**
- Global digital payments industry valued at $6.7 trillion (January 2021)
- Expected CAGR of 14% (2020-2025), reaching $10.5 trillion
- PayPal active accounts grew from 181M (2015) to 377M (2020)
- Transactions per account increased from 28 to 43 (2015-2020)

**Financial Performance:**
- Revenue growth: $13.1B (2017) → $21.5B (2020)
- Total payment volume: $936B (2020)
- Stock performance: 117% gain in 2020 vs S&P 500's 16%

**Operations:**
- 30,900+ employees globally (end of 2020)
- Operates across 193 countries and markets
- Handles 26 currencies

## 3. Key Challenges

1. **Internal Financial Wellness Crisis**: 65% of entry-level/hourly employees ran out of money between paychecks (May 2019)

2. **Mission-Reality Disconnect**: Company promoting financial health externally while employees lack financial security internally

3. **Employee Experience Gaps**: Need to maintain competitive advantage through employee satisfaction in tight labor market

4. **Stakeholder Capitalism Implementation**: Balancing shareholder returns with broader stakeholder value creation

5. **Talent Attraction/Retention**: Company previously struggled with attracting and retaining talent due to antiquated technology perception

## 4. Stakeholder Perspectives

**Dan Schulman (CEO):**
> "Our mission is to help people gain financial health. But inside our company, we didn't have financial health. Those two things are fully at odds with each other."

> "Employees are our most important stakeholder group and most critical competitive advantage. With happy employees, we can serve customers better..."

**Franz Paasche (SVP Corporate Affairs):**
> "We weren't going to do social impact from over here in a foundation; we were going to do it from the core of our business."

**John Rainey (Former CFO):**
> "If you've been a PayPal customer for five years, we can see that you've always been a good customer... So we can underwrite you and not really bear any additional risk around that."

## 5. Financial Highlights

**Revenue Trajectory:**
- 2017: $13.1B
- 2018: $15.5B  
- 2019: $17.8B
- 2020: $21.5B
- Strong consistent growth trend

**Key Metrics:**
- Active accounts: 377M (2020) vs 181M (2015)
- Transaction frequency: 43 per account (2020) vs 28 (2015)
- Total payment volume: $936B (2020)
- Stock outperformed S&P 500 significantly in 2020

**Market Context:**
- U.S. digital payment users: 78% (2020) vs 72% (2016)
- Peer-to-peer users grew 34% (2018-2021) to 90M+
- Buy-now-pay-later market: $97B (2020), expected to double by 2024

## 6. Constraints

**Timeline Pressures:**
- Financial wellness program developed and implemented within 6 months (May-November 2019)
- Need for rapid response to maintain mission credibility

**Organizational:**
- 30,900+ employee base requiring comprehensive program coverage
- Global workforce across 193 countries with varying local regulations
- Need to maintain operational performance during implementation

**Competitive:**
- Intense competition from Apple Pay, Google Pay, traditional banks, fintech startups
- Talent war in tech industry requiring competitive compensation

**Regulatory:**
- Financial services regulations across multiple jurisdictions
- Data privacy requirements (doesn't share/sell customer financial information)
