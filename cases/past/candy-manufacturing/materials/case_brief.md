## Current Situation

The client is a candy manufacturer formed through multiple mergers, resulting in a fragmented supplier base with many local suppliers for the same raw materials. Competitors are slightly larger and achieve 20% profit margins. The client's cost structure is: raw materials 70%, equipment 20%, and labor 10%, with a sales price of $1.00 per unit and current costs of $0.90 per unit.

## Key Challenges

The client's raw material costs are significantly higher than competitors, driving lower profit margins. The company needs to identify why raw material costs are elevated and determine what cost reductions are necessary to match competitor profitability.
