## Current Situation

InsureCo is a large multinational insurance provider with revenues exceeding $25B in 2009. The company operates across multiple business segments including Life Insurance and Property and Casualty (P&C) insurance in various geographies. Two primary divestiture candidates have been identified: LifeLineAsia (Asian life insurance, $250MM revenue) and All-World Property and Casualty (US-based P&C, $100MM revenue). Deloitte Consulting was engaged to assess divestiture candidates and develop an execution strategy for operational separation.

## Key Challenges

InsureCo's profitability has declined from 10% to 5% due to economic downturn and credit market tightening. The company needs to divest business units to raise over $3B in capital and refocus on core General Insurance operations. Management must identify which business units to divest to maximize sale price while optimizing portfolio composition.
