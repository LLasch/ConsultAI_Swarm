## Current Situation

The client has operated for close to 90 years, originally founded in the whiskey business but has diversified over time into a multi-million dollar operation with whiskey comprising less than 5% of revenue. The US whiskey market has been declining at 2% annually for 20 years, with the client's brand historically matching this decline rate. Recent events prompted management to focus attention on the underperforming whiskey brand, which still sells 10 million bottles annually. The company faces both internal factors (marketing spend, product quality, availability) and external factors (competitor actions, negative PR, pricing dynamics) that may explain the accelerated decline.

## Key Challenges

A 90-year-old spirits company's whiskey brand has declined 15% year-over-year despite the overall market declining only 2%, while the brand represents less than 5% of company sales. The company must understand the causes of this accelerated decline and develop a strategy to restore growth without sacrificing profitability.
