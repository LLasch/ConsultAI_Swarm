## Current Situation

The region is home to Russia's leading tourist destinations, primarily sun and beach tourism. Historical data shows tourist numbers have increased by approximately one-third over two years, but total tourism revenue has only increased by one-fifth. The government derives revenue from tourism primarily through taxation on consumption (VAT, communal services) rather than direct ownership of tourist infrastructure like hotels and beaches. The case is set in 2005 with elections scheduled for 2008.

## Key Challenges

A southern Russian region's government is dissatisfied with tourism sector revenues and has hired a consulting firm to improve performance before the 2008 elections. Analysis reveals that while tourist volume is increasing, average revenue per tourist is declining, indicating the region is not maximizing profits from its finite capacity.
