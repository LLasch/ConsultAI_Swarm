## Current Situation

Heartcorp is a medical devices company specializing in cardiovascular stents. They have developed Device X, a first-of-its-kind revolutionary product positioned to replace existing stents in the market. The company is 1 year ahead of competition in R&D and plans to launch in Europe first, followed by the U.S. within 6 months. The market involves multiple stakeholders including hospitals, insurance payers, patients, and physicians.

## Key Challenges

Heartcorp has developed Device X, a revolutionary cardiovascular stent product, and must determine the optimal pricing strategy for its European and U.S. market launch. The company faces a gap between the value-based cost neutral point and hospital customers' willingness to pay, requiring a strategic pricing and communication approach.
