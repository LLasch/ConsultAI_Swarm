## Current Situation

Set in India in 2000, Sunco is a dominant NBFC in Southern India but has minimal presence in other regions. The company has maintained an AAA rating for borrowings consistently for 5 years, which prompted the banking regulator's invitation. The commercial banking industry comprises 17 players (12 government-owned, 5 private sector), while the NBFC segment has 3 major players controlling approximately 90% of the market. Commercial banks benefit from offering checking accounts (0% interest liability products) which are highly profitable despite transaction costs, a product NBFCs are prohibited from offering.

## Key Challenges

Sunco, India's 2nd largest NBFC, has been invited by the banking regulator to convert into a commercial bank. The CEO must evaluate whether this strategic shift is a viable opportunity, considering the implications of entering the commercial banking segment and the competitive landscape.
