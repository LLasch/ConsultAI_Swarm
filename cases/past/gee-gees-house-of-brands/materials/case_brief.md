# Case Brief: Gee & Gee's House of Brands

**Industry:** cpg

**Tags:** portfolio_rationalization, market_leadership, brand_divestiture, operational_efficiency, multinational_cpg, strategic_focus

## Current Situation

Gee & Gee is a large, multinational CPG company similar to P&G or Unilever with approximately $6B in annual sales. The company operates a diverse 10-brand portfolio spanning beauty, personal care products, baby care, cleaning supplies, and pet food categories. The company's strategic intent is to exit any industry or brand where it does not hold a market leadership position, enabling greater operational nimbleness in responding to consumer trends.

## Key Challenges

Gee & Gee, a large multinational CPG company with $6B in annual sales, needs to divest half of its 10-brand portfolio to boost growth, simplify operations, improve agility, and reduce costs. The key challenge is determining which 5 brands to eliminate while maintaining market leadership positions and achieving growth targets.

## Decision

The recommended divestiture strategy focuses on eliminating brands lacking market leadership: Downward-Facing Dog Food, Bird of Paradise Feed, Headstand Hair Color, Soapvasana, and Sun Salutations Soak. The analysis prioritizes market share and consumer brand perception over raw sales size.
