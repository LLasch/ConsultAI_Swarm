## Current Situation

The American Southwest Preservation Trust is a nonprofit organization dedicated to promoting conservation and eco-tourism in the American Southwest. Coyotes, medium-sized canine carnivores native to the region, have historically maintained stable populations but have experienced dramatic recent decline. The trust has identified that habitat loss from development (highways and cities) has fragmented the coyote population into three geographically isolated groups, each with different demographic characteristics. No other fauna or flora in the administered area have been similarly impacted.

## Key Challenges

The coyote population in the American Southwest has declined significantly from 25,000 to 10,000 animals. The American Southwest Preservation Trust needs to understand why this decline is occurring and what actions should be taken to reverse it, particularly given that coyotes are a major tourist draw for the region.
