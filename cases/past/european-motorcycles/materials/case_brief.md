## Current Situation

The company is a private equity-owned German manufacturer of sporty motorcycles with current operations limited to Europe and no US presence. The US motorcycle market sells approximately 100,000 units annually, with 30% in the high-end price segment ($20K+) and 10% in the sporty category. The PE firm aims for a quick exit and is cost-conscious about capital deployment.

## Key Challenges

A German motorcycle manufacturer owned by a private equity firm seeks to expand into the US market but must decide between two distribution models: establishing a flagship dealership or selling through existing US dealership channels. The firm needs to evaluate both the strategic desirability of US expansion and which market entry approach would be optimal.
