## Current Situation

Performance Chemicals is a $250 million division of a $7 billion specialty chemicals company. The division produces asphalt additives, paper pulp bleaching solutions, and sodium solutions primarily for the North American market. Current growth trends are 3-4% annually, matching industry growth. The market is highly competitive with two major rivals and many smaller competitors, characterized by high customer and supplier power, high entry barriers, and low differentiation potential due to commoditized products.

## Key Challenges

The CEO has set an ambitious 10-15% annual growth target for all divisions, but the Performance Chemicals business manager is skeptical that significant untapped potential exists in their mature markets. The division must develop a viable growth strategy to support the corporate growth agenda while operating in a highly competitive, commodity-driven industry.
