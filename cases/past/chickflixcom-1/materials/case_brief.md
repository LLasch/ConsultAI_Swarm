## Current Situation

Chickflix.com operates an online DVD rental service with three subscription models (S1, S2, S3) targeting different customer segments. Between 2008 and 2010, the company grew its customer base from 100K to 254K and revenues from $20M to $42M. However, cost per rental ($1.85) and customer usage patterns (6-8 rentals per month) create profitability challenges. S3 achieves 30% gross margin, S2 is profitable but lower margin, while S1 operates at a loss due to underpricing relative to content costs.

## Key Challenges

Chickflix.com has achieved healthy revenue growth from $20M (2008) to $42M (2010) across three subscription tiers, but financial analysis reveals that Subscription 1 is being sold at a loss (-5% gross margin) while profitability varies significantly across tiers. The company must determine how to restructure its pricing model to eliminate losses while maintaining customer growth.
