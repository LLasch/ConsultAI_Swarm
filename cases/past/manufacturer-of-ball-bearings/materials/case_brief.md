# Case Brief: Manufacturer of Ball Bearings

**Company:** Ball Bearings Manufacturer (unnamed)
**Industry:** manufacturing
**Source:** Haas_2006.pdf (pages 22–22)
**Tags:** revenue_diversification, manufacturing, procurement, service_business, supplier_management, b2b

---

## Current Situation

The ball bearings manufacturer is part of a larger parent company and maintains strong relationships with major manufacturing clients such as Caterpillar and John Deere. The company possesses a world-class procurement department with advanced processes and personnel, plus access to 2,000 buyers from the parent company. Large manufacturing customers typically purchase from around 500 suppliers but maintain strategic relationships with only the top 20, realizing significant cost savings through these partnerships.

## Key Challenges

The company has experienced flat sales and declining profits over three years. The CEO seeks to leverage existing core competencies to build high-margin service businesses that can reverse the decline.

## Decision

Deloitte Consulting recommended a new service business model based on the client's procurement competency. The company will manage suppliers ranked below the top 20 for major strategic customers by leveraging its web-enabled electronic linkages to reduce costs and streamline the procurement process.

## Outcome

The business model is currently in the planning phase. No financial results or revenue projections are disclosed.

## Discussion Questions

- From a value chain perspective, what are the sustainable service business opportunities for this manufacturer, and how can the company build competitive advantage around them?
- How can the company effectively monetize its procurement competency and supplier relationships without cannibalizing existing business or conflicting with core customers?
