# Case Brief: Major Magazine Publisher

**Company:** Major Magazine Publisher
**Industry:** media
**Source:** Wharton_2010.pdf (pages 66–69)
**Tags:** launch, growth_strategy, magazine, market_analysis, cannibalization, cost_synergies, media_publishing

---

## Current Situation

The magazine publisher operates in a mature, non-growing market dominated by GQ (60% market share) with fragmented competition making up the remaining 40%. The company has an existing Finance magazine in its portfolio. Survey data indicates the new magazine could capture 5% of the total market valued at $262.2M, generating potential revenue of $13.16M annually.

## Key Challenges

A major magazine publisher is considering launching a new magazine and must decide whether to proceed based on market size and profitability analysis. The company needs to determine if the new magazine can achieve its $10M annual revenue goal and whether potential cannibalization of existing titles justifies the investment.

## Decision

The client should move forward with the launch of the new magazine, as projected revenue of $13.6M per year exceeds the stated goal of $10M annually, provided costs remain below $3.6M.

## Outcome

Expected to generate $13.6M in annual revenue, which exceeds the $10M target. Profitability depends on keeping total costs (fixed and variable) below $3.6M annually.

## Discussion Questions

- What are the key cost drivers for launching and operating the new magazine, and how can the publisher achieve the necessary cost structure?
- How significant is the risk of cannibalization of the Finance magazine, and what strategies can mitigate revenue overlap between the two titles?
