## Current Situation

The Coffee Grind manufactures coffeemakers and operates other divisions including dishwashers and cooking appliances. The company sells approximately 800,000 coffeemakers annually at an average retail price of $60 with a 40% margin. The dishwashers and cooking appliances division has already launched a website, prompting consideration of whether a similar channel could work for coffeemakers.

## Key Challenges

The company's coffeemaker division is experiencing margin compression due to national retailers squeezing prices. Management is exploring whether launching a direct-to-consumer website could help recover margins and reduce retailer dependency.
