## Current Situation

The client is a major telecom service provider with 50% market share in a 100M person market. Competitors include Firm A (30% share) and Firm B (20% share). The market is growing at 1% annually. Current churn rate is 3%, but recession is expected to increase it to 4.5%.

## Key Challenges

A recessionary environment is expected to increase customer churn by 50% as customers migrate to cheaper service providers. The company needs to determine how many customers to acquire from competitors to maintain its current subscriber base of 50 million.

## Decision

Not specified

## Outcome

The firm needs to steal 1.75M customers from competitors to maintain 50M subscribers. Recommendations include customized price plans, defection prediction models, loyalty programs, improved coverage, referral programs, and personalized customer communications.

## Discussion Questions

- What can our client do to decrease the churn rate?
- How many customers should the firm steal from its competitors to maintain the current number of subscribers?
