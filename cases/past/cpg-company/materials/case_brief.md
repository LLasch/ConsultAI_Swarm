## Current Situation

The client is a major consumer packaged goods company with both beverage and food portfolios. Their beverage portfolio includes sodas, waters, juices, teas, health drinks, and specialty bottled drinks. Their food portfolio consists primarily of snack foods across meal occasions. They face one major competitor in beverages who lacks a food portfolio. The food service market is segmented into cafeterias/workplace, restaurants (70% of market), entertainment venues, and miscellaneous channels.

## Key Challenges

A large CPG company with 40% market share in traditional grocery channels but only 17% in food service wants to grow its food service business. The company needs to develop a structured approach to increase market share in this fragmented and competitive market.

## Discussion Questions

- How should the company prioritize among food service segments (fast food, slow fast food, cafeteria, entertainment) given its portfolio strengths and competitive positioning?
- What are the key differences in customer needs, purchasing processes, and competitive dynamics across restaurant subsegments, and how should the company's go-to-market strategy differ?
- How can the company leverage its unique combined food and beverage portfolio as a competitive advantage against beverage-only competitors in different food service channels?
