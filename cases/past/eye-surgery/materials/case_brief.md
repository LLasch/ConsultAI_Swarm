## Current Situation

The client manufactures equipment that complements Lasik procedures, including measurement machines and post-operative adjustment lasers. The global market for these complementary devices is mature with slowing growth. The target acquisition produces intra-ocular devices used in two major categories: cataracts treatment and refractive surgery (vision correction). The refractive surgery market is fragmented and growing rapidly, while the cataracts market is larger but subject to government price caps and intense competition from major national players.

## Key Challenges

A manufacturer of complementary eye surgery equipment (measurement machines and post-operative lasers) operates in a market growing at a declining rate. The company is considering acquiring an intra-ocular lens device manufacturer to enter a higher-growth market segment and must decide which patient segment (cataracts or refractive surgery) to prioritize.
