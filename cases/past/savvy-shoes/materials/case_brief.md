## Current Situation

Savvy Shoes is a private, family-owned manufacturer of mid-price casual leather shoes for men and women, with 80 years of history. The company currently holds 30% of the US wholesale shoe market ($150M in annual sales) with 50% gross margins and owns all manufacturing facilities in China. The US shoe retail market is mature at $1B with only 5% annual growth, highly fragmented with major distribution through department stores. Current EBIT is approximately $75M with projected 3-year EBIT of $86.82M under the status quo.

## Key Challenges

Savvy Shoes, a US-based casual leather shoe manufacturer, wants to double its EBIT within 3 years to maximize the company's selling price before exit. The company must identify and evaluate growth strategies to achieve this ambitious financial target while operating in a mature, slow-growing market.
