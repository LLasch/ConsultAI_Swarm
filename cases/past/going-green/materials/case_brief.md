# Case Brief: Going Green

**Company:** North Carolina Tobacco Company
**Industry:** cannabis
**Source:** Fuqua-Case-book-2014.pdf (pages 99–111)
**Tags:** market_entry, diversification, cannabis, buy_vs_build, regulatory_environment

---

## Current Situation

North Carolina Tobacco Company generates $8 billion in annual revenue and $2 billion in profits. The company currently grows and processes tobacco products sold through retailers. Declining cigarette use in the US has prompted diversification efforts. Several states have legalized recreational marijuana sales through dispensaries, which operate under state-imposed licensing caps. The company is not concerned with federal regulations for this case.

## Key Challenges

NC Tobacco has experienced declining cigarette sales (25% of revenue) in the United States and is considering entering the recreational marijuana market to diversify its product portfolio. The company must determine whether and how to enter the emerging cannabis market in states where it is legal.
