## Current Situation

The company is the 3rd largest beer manufacturer in the UK market with 20% market share, competing against a leader with 27% and a second-place competitor with 22%. The company sells beer primarily through pubs and restaurants, with some retail distribution through supermarkets and grocery stores. The UK beer market is valued at approximately £18 billion annually, based on estimated consumption of 9 billion pints per year across 50+ million beer drinkers.

## Key Challenges

A leading beer company's profits are declining while competitors' profits are increasing. Sales volume has dropped 10% over the past year despite stable pricing, indicating a loss of market share in a competitive industry.
