# Case Brief: Bus Operator

**Company:** US Bus Transport Operator (unnamed)
**Industry:** transportation
**Source:** Haas_2006.pdf (pages 30–30)
**Tags:** cost_reduction, transportation, profitability_analysis, turnaround, operational_efficiency

---

## Current Situation

The company operates three types of routes (short, medium, long-distance) with more than half revenue from long-distance trips. It maintains substantial real estate investments in terminals and operates aging equipment. The previous CEO focused on top-line growth with initiatives like anywhere pickup service. The company's largest cost driver is fuel, and cash position is critically tight.

## Key Challenges

A US bus operator has lost money for several years due to over-operated and recently added unprofitable routes. The new CEO must aggressively cut costs to return to profitability. The company faces a tight cash position and recent brush with bankruptcy.

## Decision

A new CEO takes charge and determines the company needs to aggressively cut costs. The framework involves analyzing fixed costs (buses, stations, salaried employees) and variable costs (fuel, hourly employees, maintenance), with recommendations including route profitability analysis using load factor metrics, equipment leasing to reduce maintenance, operational efficiency improvements, and realignment of service offerings with customer needs.

## Outcome

Not disclosed
